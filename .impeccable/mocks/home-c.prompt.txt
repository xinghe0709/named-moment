Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web application north-star comp, 1440×1024 landscape, compositional option 3
Primary request: Design the first viewport of the Chinese web product “此刻有名”. A person writes a private difficult-to-name moment and AI later matches it to three real sourced concepts. Preserve the committed world of a steel-framed hand-rolled glass partition, but make the experience read as a left-to-right journey through glass rather than a centered card or standard hero.
Surface structure: no sidebar, no floating cards, no conventional landing-page hero. A compact brand pane in the upper-left says “此刻有名”; a compact upper-right pane links “私人档案馆”. The main elevation has three vertical bays: the first narrow bay is a quiet prompt pane saying “有些感受，先发生，后来才有名字。”; the middle bay is by far the widest and contains the heading “告诉我一个你无法解释的瞬间。” over a large clear borderless writing area with placeholder “写下一段日记、一个念头，或刚刚发生的事……” and character note “至少写下 10 个字”; the third narrow bay is a deep cobalt translucent pane with the action “为此刻寻找名字”, vertically centered and unmistakably clickable. A thin amber line glows only at the seam between writing and action. Along the lower edge, three small clear panes quietly preview the future result structure with labels “01 读懂你的此刻” “02 找到三个名字” “03 收藏最像的一个”, but the writing area remains dominant.
Style/medium: exact polished UI/UX mockup, front-on architectural elevation, quiet contemporary glazier workshop, crisp accessible consumer interface, no people, no photography, no illustrative icons.
Typography: confident humanist grotesque with excellent Chinese legibility, large scale contrast, no all-caps micro labels, no monospace decoration, no display serif.
Color palette: matte mullion black #171918, seeded glass white #E9ECE7, cool atmospheric glass #C9D6D3, memory cobalt #1646B8, tiny restrained amber #F2A93B. No beige, terracotta, neon, purple, or gradients.
Materials/textures: subtle watery seeded glass, true 5–7px black glazing bars, diffuse daylight behind glass, almost flat. No glassmorphism cards, no shadows behind generic containers.
Composition/framing: full-bleed desktop browser viewport, strong horizontal journey, asymmetrical column widths, the next wall bay barely visible below fold.
Text (verbatim): “此刻有名” “私人档案馆” “有些感受，先发生，后来才有名字。” “告诉我一个你无法解释的瞬间。” “写下一段日记、一个念头，或刚刚发生的事……” “至少写下 10 个字” “为此刻寻找名字” “01 读懂你的此刻” “02 找到三个名字” “03 收藏最像的一个”
Constraints: interface only, no browser chrome, no device frame, no invented statistics, no testimonials, no generic cards, no rounded rectangles, no pill buttons, no excessive icons, no decorative arrows, no watermark.
