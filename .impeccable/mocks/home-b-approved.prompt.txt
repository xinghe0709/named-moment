Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop web application north-star comp, 1440×1024 landscape, compositional option 2
Primary request: Design the first viewport of the Chinese web product “此刻有名”. A person describes an emotionally difficult-to-name moment; AI will return three real, sourced words from world languages. Preserve the committed world of a steel-framed hand-rolled glass partition, but explore a different topology from a simple left-content/right-copy split.
Surface structure: no conventional header/hero/cards/sidebar. A slim top steel transom contains “此刻有名” left and “私人档案馆” right. The main viewport is an irregular 3×3 glazing elevation. The largest central pane spans two columns and two rows and is an unmistakable writing surface, with “告诉我一个你无法解释的瞬间。” at the top, a borderless large textarea area, placeholder “写下一段日记、一个念头，或刚刚发生的事……”, and a black glazing-bar action “为此刻寻找名字” embedded across its bottom edge. Around it are narrow museum-label panes: one tall clear pane carries the promise “这种感觉，世界上也许早已有名字。”; one small cobalt pane carries “真实概念”; one small amber pane carries “可靠来源”; one frosted pane shows “每次返回三个接近的答案”. The grid itself should read like an intimate museum wall, not a dashboard.
Style/medium: exact polished UI/UX mockup, straight-on architectural elevation, contemporary glazier studio, crisp consumer interface, no people and no photography. The textarea and action must be instantly recognizable.
Typography: confident humanist grotesque with excellent Chinese legibility, scale contrast only; no all-caps micro labels, no monospace decoration, no display serif.
Color palette: matte mullion black #171918, seeded glass white #E9ECE7, cool atmospheric glass #C9D6D3, memory cobalt #1646B8, restrained amber #F2A93B. No beige, terracotta, neon, purple, or gradients.
Materials/textures: subtle watery seeded glass, varied translucent panes, true 5–7px black bars, diffuse backlight, almost flat. No glassmorphism cards and no floating UI chrome.
Composition/framing: full-bleed browser viewport, asymmetrical but balanced, generous clear areas, small glimpse of continuing wall below fold.
Text (verbatim): “此刻有名” “私人档案馆” “告诉我一个你无法解释的瞬间。” “写下一段日记、一个念头，或刚刚发生的事……” “为此刻寻找名字” “这种感觉，世界上也许早已有名字。” “真实概念” “可靠来源” “每次返回三个接近的答案”
Constraints: interface only, no browser chrome, no device frame, no invented statistics, no testimonials, no extra navigation, no generic cards, no rounded rectangles, no pill buttons, no excessive icons, no decorative arrows, no watermark.
